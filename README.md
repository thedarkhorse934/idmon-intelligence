# IDMON — Digital Asset Intelligence

IDMON is a personal Bitcoin intelligence and research project focused on turning raw blockchain and market data into **reproducible analytical evidence**.

This repository is a curated, public-safe portfolio of selected investigation and research outputs. It is **not** a mirror of the live IDMON production codebase.

## Focus

- Bitcoin transaction intelligence
- On-chain investigations and UTXO lineage analysis
- Reproducible evidence and provenance
- System-evaluation and detector-gap analysis
- Bitcoin market-structure and derivatives research

## Analytical principles

IDMON investigations distinguish between:

`ON_CHAIN_FACT` · `IDMON_EXISTING_LABEL` · `OBSERVED_PATTERN` · `HEURISTIC_INFERENCE` · `UNRESOLVED`

Core principles:

- Evidence before narrative.
- Exact transaction and outpoint references where available.
- Reproducible calculations and explicit provenance.
- Movement does not by itself establish sale, ownership change or criminal activity.
- Attribution requires independent evidence.
- Uncertainty remains visible.

## Status

IDMON is an active independent research project. The material published here is selected for clarity, reproducibility and professional review rather than completeness.

Research outputs are analytical work, not financial advice or trade recommendations.
