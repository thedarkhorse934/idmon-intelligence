# IDMON Research

This area is reserved for selected public-safe research notes from IDMON.

Current internal research spans Bitcoin market structure, derivatives state, prospective hypothesis testing, setup discovery and system evaluation. Only material that can be presented clearly and without exposing private runtime data, credentials, execution configuration or internal operational details will be published here.

## Publication principles

- Prospective evidence is preferred over retrospective storytelling.
- Research candidates are not presented as validated trading edges.
- Observation is kept separate from prediction.
- Invalidation, sample limits and failed hypotheses remain visible.
- Research outputs are not financial advice or trade recommendations.

Additional research notes will be added selectively as they become publication-ready.
